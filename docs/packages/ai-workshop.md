# AI literacy for your team

**Phase 3 — Keep it running, bring the team · 1 day · fixed price**

## Positioning

For the team that is already using AI — except nobody is quite sure what it can do,
what it can't, and what's allowed with company data.

A day on site, tailored to the work the team actually does. No slides about how AI
is changing the world: hands-on work with the team's own documents and processes,
and clear ground rules by the end. The workshop sits in phase 3 deliberately — it's
what makes everything built in phase 2 land with the people who have to use it.

**Promise:** After one day your team knows what AI can and can't do — and where it
actually pays off in your work.

## Deliverables

### 1. The day itself

- **On site, tailored** — the exercises use the team's own documents and processes,
  prepared in a short intake beforehand. Everyone builds something that works before
  the day ends.
- **Plain-language fundamentals** — how a language model works, why it sometimes
  produces confident nonsense, and how to spot it.

### 2. Ground rules

- **What is and isn't allowed with company data** — practical rules the team can
  apply the next morning, aligned with the GDPR and the AI Act. Not a legal
  document; a one-pager people actually follow.

### 3. The use-case list

- **A prioritised list of use cases the team came up with themselves** — which is
  what makes it stick: they're the team's ideas, not a consultant's. The list is a
  direct feeder for the [Process scan](process-scan.md) (to verify realism) or the
  [AI prototype](ai-prototype.md) (to build the best one).

## The day

| When | Activity |
|---|---|
| Morning | What it is: how a language model works, why it sometimes produces confident nonsense, how to spot it — in plain language |
| Afternoon | Hands on: working with the team's own documents and processes; everyone builds something that works |
| Closing | Ground rules and next steps: what's allowed with company data, and the prioritised use-case list |

## Explicitly out of scope

- Building any of the use cases — that's the [AI prototype](ai-prototype.md).
- Formal GDPR/AI Act compliance advice — the ground rules are practical guidance,
  not legal counsel.

## Bridges

- Works standalone as a low-threshold entry point — for some organisations this is
  the first engagement, before any scan.
- The use-case list feeds the [Process scan](process-scan.md) (are these realistic?)
  and the [AI prototype](ai-prototype.md) (build the winner).
- Pairs with phase-2 deliveries: a workshop scheduled alongside a
  [dashboard](dashboards.md) or [warehouse](data-warehouse.md) handover
  raises adoption of both.
